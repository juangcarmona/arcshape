## Selected practical tips

- [Give a compact summary of requirements and driving forces!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/1-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`01-introduction-and-goals/README.md`](./01-introduction-and-goals/README.md).
- [Describe only the top 3-5 quality goals in the introduction!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/1-16/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`01-introduction-and-goals/README.md`](./01-introduction-and-goals/README.md).
- [Search broadly for stakeholders!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/1-19/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`01-introduction-and-goals/README.md`](./01-introduction-and-goals/README.md).
- [Clarify the consequences of constraints!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/2-2/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`02-constraints/README.md`](./02-constraints/README.md).
- [Differentiate different categories of constraints!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/2-5/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`02-constraints/README.md`](./02-constraints/README.md).
- [Explicitly demarcate your system from its environment!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/3-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`03-context-and-scope/README.md`](./03-context-and-scope/README.md).
- [Combine the context diagram with a table!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/3-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`03-context-and-scope/README.md`](./03-context-and-scope/README.md).
- [Show all (all!) external interfaces!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/3-9/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`03-context-and-scope/README.md`](./03-context-and-scope/README.md).
- [Differentiate business and technical context!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/3-10/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`03-context-and-scope/README.md`](./03-context-and-scope/README.md).
- [Explain the solution strategy as compact as possible (e.g. as list of keywords)!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/4-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`04-solution-strategy/README.md`](./04-solution-strategy/README.md).
- [Describe solution approaches in context of quality requirements!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/4-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`04-solution-strategy/README.md`](./04-solution-strategy/README.md).
- [Justify the solution strategy!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/4-6/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`04-solution-strategy/README.md`](./04-solution-strategy/README.md).
- [Organize the building block view hierarchically!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/5-2/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`05-building-block-view/README.md`](./05-building-block-view/README.md).
- [Always describe level-1 of the building block view ('Level-1 is your friend')!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/5-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`05-building-block-view/README.md`](./05-building-block-view/README.md).
- [Describe the responsibility or purpose of every (important) blackbox!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/5-5/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`05-building-block-view/README.md`](./05-building-block-view/README.md).
- [Explain the mapping of source-code to building blocks!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/5-13/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`05-building-block-view/README.md`](./05-building-block-view/README.md).
- [Always map existing building blocks to the activities within runtime scenarios!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/6-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`06-runtime-view/README.md`](./06-runtime-view/README.md).
- [Document only a few runtime scenarios!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/6-2/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`06-runtime-view/README.md`](./06-runtime-view/README.md).
- [Document 'schematic' (instead of detailed) scenarios!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/6-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`06-runtime-view/README.md`](./06-runtime-view/README.md).
- [Document the various environments!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/7-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`07-deployment-view/README.md`](./07-deployment-view/README.md).
- [Document the deployment view hierarchically!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/7-4/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`07-deployment-view/README.md`](./07-deployment-view/README.md).
- [Document the mapping of building-blocks to hardware!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/7-5/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`07-deployment-view/README.md`](./07-deployment-view/README.md).
- [Explain what (else) is relevant for productive use (aka operation) of the system!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/7-9/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`07-deployment-view/README.md`](./07-deployment-view/README.md).
- [Explain the Concepts!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/8-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md`](./08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md).
- [Restrict documentation of concepts to the most important topics!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/8-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md`](./08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md).
- [In concepts, explain HOW it works!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/8-4/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md`](./08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md).
- [(Hyper)Link between Building Blocks and Concepts!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/8-11/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md`](./08-crosscutting-concepts/README.md).
- [Document only architecturally relevant decisions!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`09-architecture-decisions/README.md`](./09-architecture-decisions/README.md).
- [Document decision criteria!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-2/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`09-architecture-decisions/README.md`](./09-architecture-decisions/README.md).
- [Provide reasons for important decisions!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-3/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`09-architecture-decisions/README.md`](./09-architecture-decisions/README.md).
- [Document decisions as Architecture Decision Record (ADR)!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/9-5/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`09-architecture-decisions/README.md`](./09-architecture-decisions/README.md).
- [Keep the quality goals in arc42-section 1.2 short!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/10-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`10-quality-requirements/README.md`](./10-quality-requirements/README.md).
- [Consider usage or application (quality) scenarios!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/10-5/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`10-quality-requirements/README.md`](./10-quality-requirements/README.md).
- [Consider change (quality) scenarios!!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/10-6/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`10-quality-requirements/README.md`](./10-quality-requirements/README.md).
- [Use (quality) scenarios for architecture analysis or evaluation!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/10-8/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`10-quality-requirements/README.md`](./10-quality-requirements/README.md).
- [Search for problems and risks with different stakeholders!](https://docs.arc42.org/tips/11-1/) — Gernot Starke, CC BY-SA 4.0; linked from [`11-risks-and-technical-debt/README.md`](./11-risks-and-technical-debt/README.md).
