## Definition

The accepted ProductShape definition that records an adopting repository's product intent and requirements.

## Distinguish From

It is not architecture documentation or an OpenSpec delivery change. It remains the authority for product intent when ArcShape connects to it.

## Usage

When present, ArcShape may discover and cite approved product artifacts. The first-release extent of that integration remains an open question.
