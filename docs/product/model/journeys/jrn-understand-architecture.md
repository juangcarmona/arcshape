## Intended Outcome

The reader understands the system's architecture well enough to answer relevant questions about it.

## Entry Conditions

Architecture documentation and a reader-facing view are available to the reader.

## Journey Narrative

The reader opens the architecture view, explores information relevant to their question, and uses the documentation to form an understanding of the system.

## Variants and Branches

The reader may be technical or non-technical and may need different levels of detail. The first-release comprehension and accessibility expectations remain open.

## Completion Conditions

The reader can find and understand the relevant architecture information; the product's evaluation threshold remains to be decided.
