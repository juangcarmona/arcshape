## Requirement

The architecture view must help non-technical readers understand the system's purpose, major components, and important relationships.

## Measurement

Evaluate representative readers with a task-based comprehension review against agreed questions about a sample system. The question set, audience coverage, and pass threshold require product-owner decisions before this quality requirement can be accepted.
