## Purpose

Needs to understand a software system's purpose, structure, and important relationships from its architecture documentation.

## Goals

- Find an understandable explanation of the system.
- Understand the architecture at a level appropriate to their role and background.

## Responsibilities

- Uses architecture documentation to answer questions about the system.
- Provides feedback when the documentation is unclear or incomplete.

## Boundaries

Readers consume the documentation; they do not author or approve the repository's product definition by virtue of reading it.
